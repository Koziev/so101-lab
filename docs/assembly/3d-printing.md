# 3D printing

This page will collect print settings and observations for SO-101 components.

## What to record

For each part, document:

- Source model and revision (link to the original project or repository).
- Printer and nozzle diameter.
- Filament manufacturer, material, and color.
- Layer height, wall count, infill, supports, and orientation.
- Print time and material usage.
- Fit, strength, warping, and post-processing observations.
